# Blinkit-Data-Analysis-Dashboard


**Project Overview**
This project showcases a detailed Power BI dashboard built to analyze Blinkit’s operational performance. It provides visual insights into sales, product distribution, outlet segmentation, and customer satisfaction metrics. The dashboard empowers data-driven decisions through clear and interactive visuals.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
The dashboard highlights the following major KPIs:

1.Total Sales: $1.20M in total revenue from all product categories

2.Average Sales: $141 per item on average

3.Number of Items: 8,523 distinct items recorded in sales

4.Average Rating: 3.9 out of 5 based on customer feedback

**Features**

1.Interactive Filters: Enables data slicing by outlet location type, outlet size (small, medium, high), and item type.

2.Outlet Establishment Trend: Displays a line graph of outlet growth from 2010 to 2022, peaking at $205K in 2019.

3.Fat Content Analysis: Compares sales between low-fat and regular products with a breakdown by outlet tiers (Tier 1 to Tier 3).

4.Item Type Sales Breakdown: Highlights top-selling categories like fruits, snacks, and household items, with the lowest in seafood and breakfast bars.

5.Outlet Size & Location Performance: Shows medium-sized outlets leading in sales and Tier 3 locations generating the highest revenue at $472.13K.

6.Outlet Type Metrics Table: Compares outlet types (e.g., grocery stores, supermarkets) on total sales, item count, average sales, ratings, and item visibility.

**Insights and Conclusions**

1.Robust Sales: Blinkit achieved over $1.2M in total sales, indicating healthy growth and product demand.

2.Health Trends: A strong preference for low-fat products reveals a health-conscious consumer base.

3.Top Categories: Fruits, snacks, and frozen foods are top-selling categories.

4.Outlet Size Effect: Medium outlets consistently outperform both small and high-sized ones in revenue generation.

5.Tier-Wise Analysis: Tier 3 cities show the strongest performance, suggesting untapped potential in non-metro areas.

6.Visibility Metric: Grocery stores exhibit the best item visibility score (0.10), which may correlate with higher customer engagement.
